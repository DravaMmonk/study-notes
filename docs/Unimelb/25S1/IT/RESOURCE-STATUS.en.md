## Items that are incomplete or need to be kept apart

- **The Transport Layer (1) and (2) slides were not recovered.** The Week 8 module list has no lecture entry and the Week 9 page has only Transport Layer (3). Transport Layer (3)'s printed numbers start at 57, so the two earlier decks had about 55 slides between them. [09](09-transport-layer-udp-tcp.en.md) therefore uses the transport pages of `Week12-Review-Lecture3.pdf` as its slide source, supplemented by 16 screenshots from the student's notes (`note-01`–`note-16`, taken from the weekly notes "WEEK 8 - Transport Layer", "WEEK 8-9 - Transport2 - UDP & TCP" and "WEEK 9 - Transport3 - TCP Management"). They come from the unrecovered decks or from photos of in-class annotations, cannot be given PDF page numbers, and their resolution is that of the student's originals; one carries a handwritten slip by the student, which is flagged in the note.
- **Tutorial materials are not held locally.** This recovery took only the files on the lecture pages; the Canvas tutorial pages, questions and solutions were not downloaded, and there are no assignment or exam files locally either.
- **File names that do not match content.** `Week9-Transport Layer-Lectures2and3.pdf` opens with Transport Layer (3); the two `Week4-Lectures23-*.pdf` files are Data Link Layer (3) and MAC Sub-Layer (1); `W3-L2-example_solutions.pdf` belongs to Physical Layer (2) (printed pages 45–47); `example_solutions.pdf` has no week in its name and contains the Physical Layer (1) examples (printed pages 8–9). Filing follows the first-page title and content.
- **The Week 6 lecture order is inferred.** The three files are Network Layer (2), (3) and (4) in order, so `Week6-routing.pdf` is taken to be W6-L3; the Week 7 page states that it reuses W6-L3.
- **Weeks and part numbers in the student's notes.** The student's "Network2", "Network3" and "Transport4" are the decks Network Layer (3), (4) and Transport Layer (3); "WEEK12 - Internet Security" covers both Network Security (1) (the Week 11 file) and (2). Streaming media and email in Week 11 have no weekly student note; only the "Application Layer" review note touches them.
- **Review material contains sample questions.** Sample and practice exam questions in `Week12-Review-Lecture3.pdf`, `Week12-Review-1-sample-solution.pdf` and the review notes are used only as a source of concepts and are not reproduced; the screenshot marked Assignment-2 in the `Network Layer` review note is not published; "practice_exam" has only a heading.
- **Slips in the slides** are flagged in the relevant notes as a **Common mistake** or an `Extension`; the original files are not altered: `Week6-IP.pdf` p.13 prints `192.24.12.0/22` as `192.2412.0/22` in its figure ([07](07-ip-addressing.en.md)); `Week10-Application Layer-Lecture1.pdf` p.19 spells washington as "aashington" ([11](11-dns-and-www.en.md)); `Week11-Lectures2&3.pdf` p.20 gives the 3DES key space as $3\times2^{56}$ and AES as having "128–256-bit blocks" ([13](13-cryptography.en.md)); the title "half of previous" on `Week9-Transport Layer-Lectures2and3.pdf` p.15 means the congestion window at the time of loss and is easily misread as the old threshold ([10](10-congestion-control.en.md)). In the review answers, CFB's "previous 8 ciphertext blocks" should be the previous 8 ciphertext bytes. Slips in the student's notes (Ethernet's minimum frame size, the DNS port, the checksum's end-around carry, a NAK in Go-Back-N, and others) are flagged in the same way.

## Sync and verification

- All 26 files listed in `materials.json` were re-hashed with `shasum -a 256`; SHA-256, byte size and `pdfinfo` page count all match the record.
- All 26 PDFs are readable by `pdfinfo` and `pdftotext -layout`; all 480 pages were rendered with `pdftoppm -png -r 110` and looked through deck by deck, and the selected pages were checked again at full size for legibility.
- Printed slide numbers run on across consecutive decks of the same topic (physical layer 1–52, data link layer 1–51, MAC sublayer 2–39, network layer 2–66, application layer 1–77, security 1–58), so apart from the transport layer no pages are missing; Introduction to Networking (2) restarts at 1, so the gap between the two introduction decks cannot be checked this way.
- The Apple Notes export has 26 notes (17 weekly, 8 review, 1 empty) and 187 images; wherever a local deck exists, its screenshots were replaced by freshly rendered slide pages.
- Every image path, cross-note link and anchor in the notes resolves; the Chinese and English versions have the same heading structure, image order, anchors and number of source comments.
