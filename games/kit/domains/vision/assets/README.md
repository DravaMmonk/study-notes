# Camera experiment artwork

These transparent sprites were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool for Vision Lab, then encoded as
WebP with `cwebp -q 86`, preserving alpha. They contain no mathematical labels or light paths. The application
positions the sprites and draws all measurements from the pinhole model; changing artwork cannot change a verdict.

- `pinhole-camera.webp`: navy metal, leather bellows and brass pinhole camera, orthographic side view.
- `lighthouse.webp`: red/ivory calibration lighthouse, orthographic front view.

## Camera prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: a single transparent cutout sprite for an interactive camera optics
puzzle game. Subject: a small beautiful professional educational pinhole camera instrument, camera box body made
of deep navy blue painted metal, folded dark charcoal leather bellows, machined brass corner fittings and knurled
adjustment knobs, precise screws, mounted on a short brushed aluminium optical rail carriage. Strict SIDE VIEW,
camera faces RIGHT, small thin brass front plate on the RIGHT edge with a tiny pinhole centred vertically, no glass
lens, rear camera box LEFT. Horizontal compact body 3:2 overall aspect ratio. Style: high-end physically based 3D
product render, realistic materials and warm soft studio light, crisp polished game asset with clear recognisable
silhouette, not flat icon, not vector, not line drawing, not monochrome. Orthographic side view without perspective
so it can align to a mathematical side-view scene. Isolated single object on true transparent alpha background, no
room, no table extending outside the carriage, no floor, no baked shadow beyond object, no labels, no text, no
arrows, no rays, no measurements, no logos, no UI.

## Lighthouse prompt

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: single transparent cutout prop for an interactive optics game.
Subject: one red and ivory striped miniature lighthouse used as a recognisable photography calibration object,
a tall slender painted wood tower with realistic grain and softly worn glossy red paint, dark navy little roof,
brass lantern housing, warm yellow lamp, tiny believable balcony. Strict front orthographic elevation with flat
circular base, symmetrical simple readable silhouette. High-quality physically based 3D product render, lifelike
light and materials, warm light from upper left, crisp edges. Show the entire object centred, tall 1:2 shape, well
separated from the transparent border. No coast, no sea, no environment, no background, no ground plane, no drop
shadow outside object. No letters, numbers, labels, rulers, arrows, text, symbols, logo or UI. True transparent alpha
background. Match a realistic navy metal and brass educational camera instrument in a warm softly lit optical studio.
