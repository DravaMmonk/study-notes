    Resource Catalogue¶

    Roadmap¶

    • Advanced Machine Learning Roadmap - source roadmap and chapter order.

    Required books¶

    Pattern Recognition and Machine Learning¶

    • Author: Christopher M. Bishop
    • Microsoft Research publication page
    • Official full PDF
    • Local download: readings/books/prml-bishop-2006.pdf

    Microsoft Research explicitly provides the complete book as a PDF.

    All of Statistics¶

    • Author: Larry Wasserman
    • Springer book page
    • Local status: link only

    No author or publisher page with an explicit free full-book download was identified during resource collection. Use library or institutional access rather than third-party scans.

    Reference books¶

    Machine Learning: A Probabilistic Perspective¶

    • Author: Kevin P. Murphy
    • Author's book page and supporting material
    • Official table of contents and preface
    • Successor open book project: Probabilistic Machine Learning
    • Local download: readings/books/mlapp-toc-preface.pdf

    The original MLAPP book remains a link-only resource; the manifest downloads only the preview material hosted on the author's UBC page.

    Convex Optimization¶

    • Authors: Stephen Boyd and Lieven Vandenberghe
    • Official book page
    • Official full PDF
    • Official lecture slides
    • Stanford EE364A material
    • Local downloads: readings/books/convex-optimization.pdf and readings/slides/convex-optimization-slides.pdf

    The authors' page provides the book, slides, errata, examples, and links to related course material.

    Advanced books¶

    Graphical Models, Exponential Families, and Variational Inference¶

    • Authors: Martin J. Wainwright and Michael I. Jordan
    • Publisher page
    • Author-hosted full PDF
    • Local download: readings/books/wainwright-jordan-2008.pdf

    The author-hosted PDF is the 2008 Foundations and Trends version named by the roadmap.

    Theory of Point Estimation¶

    • Authors: E. L. Lehmann and George Casella
    • Springer book page
    • DOI: 10.1007/b98854
    • Local status: link only

    Springer lists the second edition as a paid or institution-access ebook. Third-party scans are intentionally excluded.

    CMU 10-708: Probabilistic Graphical Models¶

    • Spring 2021 course home
    • Schedule and readings
    • Spring 2020 course home
    • Spring 2019 lecture videos
    • Local downloads: the Spring 2021 schedule and 18 selected lecture slide decks under readings/cmu-10-708/

    The course covers graphical-model representation, exact inference, structured prediction, Monte Carlo methods, MCMC, Bayesian inference, variational inference, deep generative models, nonparametric Bayesian methods, Gaussian processes, and causality.

    Lectures 1-10 and 12-19 are downloaded because they directly support the roadmap's core path. Lectures 11 and 20-25 remain link-only entries in the manifest as extension material.

    Download policy¶

    • Download only files made publicly available by an author, publisher, or university course site.
    • Keep downloaded source material in the ignored readings/ directory.
    • Commit notes, the resource manifest, and the reproducible downloader.
    • Recheck the source page if a download URL stops working.
