    Vision Lab stages 0–2: construction and coverage review¶

    Date: 2026-11-04. Scope: 26 levels, 31 circuit tasks, 18 earned parts. The course replaces the executable camera-first trial. All task content and save migrations were written through the operation set. The long-term outline remains proposed beyond stage 2. This is a source, implementation and interaction review, not a measured learning study.

    Construction and source map¶

    Level IDs here are source IDs, usable with ?level=<id>. Display labels are derived from the branch topology. Original lecture PDFs take precedence over note summaries. Application models and mathematical execution helpers are declared separately from independent lecture knowledge.

    Construction/tasks Course anchor Learner relationship and transfer evidence
    0-1 signed then absolute comparison L2 PDF pp22–26; L3 point operations pp6–7 Corresponding values/channels, sign versus magnitude; brightening, darkening and RGB. Absolute value is mathematical support.
    0-2 known-offset comparison L2 image coordinates; declared fixture offset convention Sample positions change before comparison; dx/dy are given, not estimated registration. Different axes and signs.
    0-3 inspection composition Point operations; controlled task criterion Separate measured differences from tolerance decisions. Lighting, known alignment and tolerance are assumptions, not general recognition.
    1-1 weighted line L3 pp8–18,21–23 Read neighbours, match positional weights, multiply and add. Impulse, constant, asymmetric signed weights and narrow images.
    1-2 two-axis reuse; 1-5 smooth/detail application L3 pp24–28,35,37–40 Sequential passes versus adding independent responses; corners/outer products. Given normalised Gaussian weights, signed detail versus absolute change.
    1-3 general local kernel L3 pp9–18,31–35 Samples and weights remain separate until the player composes multiplication, sum and shape restoration. Identity, asymmetric and averaging rules.
    1-4 kernel orientation L3 pp17–18 Earn convolution by reversing a kernel before the player's correlation; asymmetric impulses expose a distinction hidden by symmetric kernels.
    1-6 four border/valid tasks L3 pp41–45 Replicate, periodic and edge-inclusive reflection versus zero; valid interior changes size. Independent numeric continuations precede each configuration.
    1-7 signed components; 1-8 Sobel/strength L3 pp29–30; L5 pp39–42 Axis/sign from ramps; perpendicular smoothing reuses the weighted line. Supplied length arithmetic prevents manual squaring/root calculation.
    1-9 channel measurement L3 pp31–32 Reuse weighted aggregation on channels; independent R/G/B evidence and changed channel contributions. Weights are an instrument specification.
    1-10 scanner L3 common filters/composition Actual join consumes the learner's channel measurement, two-axis filter and Sobel. Noise conditioning precedes measurement; no complete-document detection claim.
    2-1 wave; 2-2 projection L4 pp7–24 Position × cycles plus phase, then observation × basis and mean. Changed cycle counts and quarter phases; a null projection need not mean no frequency.
    2-3 quadrature; 2-4 complete row spectrum L4 pp18–26 Retain cos/−sin projections, magnitude and every discrete index. DC, shifted stripes, odd lengths and multiple rows. Repetition/indexing is a supplied service.
    2-5 synthesis; 2-6 magnitude/phase mixture L4 pp18–35 Reuse the player's wave. Forward mean versus inverse sum; coefficient magnitude is not sinusoid peak amplitude. Zero-phase direction is explicitly undefined.
    2-7 complex row reuse; 2-8 2D spectrum L4 pp25–32; complex linear arithmetic support Preserve both components between axes, restore rectangular orientation, expose magnitude. No FFT/DFT primitive.
    2-9 paired gains L4 pp38–44 Construct a symmetric low-pass criterion, scale both coefficient components, reconstruct. Low-pass, DC removal and an explicit band-pass case.
    2-10 ringing comparison L4 pp45–47 Compare hard/tapered responses on an impulse and a step using the player's frequency filter and comparator. Inspect unclipped overshoot.
    2-11 convolution equivalence L4 p37; L3 boundaries The player's periodic convolver supplies the impulse response; complex products and inverse match spatial output. Match origin, boundaries and normalisation.
    2-12 inverse 2D Fourier/inverse representation; supplied conjugation identity Reuse the 2D forward graph through conjugation and WH scaling; check imaginary residual and non-square dimensions.
    2-13 interference machine L4 2D spectra/frequency filtering; controlled known-interference model Actual join consumes 2D analysis/inversion, the row-frequency filter and the convolver. Row-only filtering cannot isolate y variation from retained row DC.

    All images retain floating-point original values. Display clipping does not change comparisons, convolution or reconstruction. The numerical fixtures were generated from direct spatial sums and complex exponential definitions, not by running the domain's row-expansion graph to manufacture its expected answers.

    Dependencies and practice decisions¶

    • Stage 0 is deliberately short and serial: absolute comparison needs the signed comparison task, alignment wraps that comparator, and inspection consumes aligned evidence. No arbitrary branch or coordinate-copying gate.
    • Stage 1 branches only after the common weighted relationship. Separable/detail, local-kernel/direction and channel routes do not consume sibling parts. The scanner consumes all three.
    • Stage 2 branches only after a complete row spectrum. The synthesis/filtering route and complex/2D route use their own constructions; the finale requires the row-frequency comparison as well as 2D processing.
    • Blueprint bp-lonely reports identify first-use points without separate practice levels. Facets in those short ladders are covered by tasks, asymmetric/constant/changed-phase tests and downstream compositions; no extra levels were added solely to clear a density report. The stage-0 ladder report reflects its short application opening.
    • Boundary tasks use the same carried board for changing continuation models. Signed-to-absolute comparison also carries the player's graph. Incomplete starters remove layout work without solving the decisive signal path.
    • Tools follow task introductions: absolute value enters the second comparison task, and valid-domain trimming enters the final boundary task. Arithmetic is one configurable component. Outside settings remain unavailable until their path introduces them; zero and replicated extension are explained at their first actual use.
    • No source point is considered mastered merely because a term appears or a reference graph passes. Camera optics, interpolation, learning and later machines remain outside this executable scope. Stride variants, a full scale/ support design ladder and additional factorisation efficiency constraints remain coverage opportunities.

    Verification and limits¶

    Mathematical checks independently cover zero/replicate/wrap/reflected reads, RGB local processing, axis settings, valid shapes, complex 2D coefficients, DC/phase, rectangular reconstruction and imaginary residuals. Packed reuse is checked with a different passed graph, including nested settings and inspector settings. New packed-node defaults are checked against their declared interfaces so hidden axis choices cannot be overwritten.

    Opening tasks were checked through part placement, tapped ports and runs. A fresh task-scoped session starts with Arithmetic alone, passes the signed task, then sees the absolute-value introduction. Later-machine UI checks use an explicitly seeded review state containing reference boards; they are not evidence that a fresh player solved the course. Emulated touch dragging moved connected tiles while SVG wire paths updated during movement. Details, axis/border controls, pixel popovers, numeric image cells, task scrolling, closed initial result drawers and the final comparison outputs are checked at recorded desktop/phone viewports.

    The check log and screenshots are local QA artifacts under output/playwright/. Automated checks establish contracts and mathematical examples, not enjoyment, novelty, novice mastery or behaviour on a physical iPhone. The independent-agent review gate is not claimed: this revision was implemented and reviewed in the current session.
